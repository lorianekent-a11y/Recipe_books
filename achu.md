Ingredients
The Base (Achu / Pounded Cocoyam):

2 to 3 lbs white cocoyams (taro roots) — Note: Yellow cocoyams are traditional, but white cocoyams are a great, easily accessible alternative.

Water for boiling

The Yellow Soup:

1.5 cups red palm oil

1 to 2 tablespoons Achu limestone (locally known as kang or canwa — this is the secret ingredient that reacts with palm oil to turn it yellow and thicken it)

1 cup ground crayfish

2 tablespoons njangsang seeds (ground/blended) — adds an incredible nutty aroma

Achu spices / Bakang (a traditional blend of local spices, optional but recommended)

Scotch bonnet peppers (to taste)

Salt and bouillon cubes to taste

2 to 3 cups warm water or meat stock

Proteins:

Smoked meat, tripe (cow stomach), cow skin (kanda), or oxtail (pre-boiled until completely tender)

Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Boil and Pound the Cocoyams
Peel the cocoyams, wash them thoroughly, and cut them into large chunks.

Place them in a large pot, cover with water, and boil on medium-high heat until they are fork-tender.

Drain any excess water. While still very hot, pound the cocoyams in a mortar with a pestle (or blend/mash them thoroughly in a heavy-duty food processor) until they form a smooth, sticky, dough-like consistency (Achu).

Mold the pounded cocoyam into smooth mounds and set them aside on serving plates.

2. Prepare the Limestone Solution
In a small bowl, dissolve the Achu limestone (canwa) in about half a cup of warm water. Let it settle for a minute, then strain the clear liquid (discarding any gritty sediment at the bottom).

3. Make the Yellow Soup Base
In a separate pot or bowl, pour your red palm oil.

Gradually pour in the strained limestone water while stirring the oil vigorously. You will notice a magical chemical reaction: the red palm oil will instantly curdle, foam slightly, and turn a bright, opaque yellow color!

4. Mix in the Spices and Meats
Add the warm meat stock or water to the yellow oil mixture, stirring continuously.

Stir in the ground crayfish, blended njangsang, Achu spices, and finely chopped scotch bonnet peppers.

Season with salt and bouillon cubes to taste.

Add your pre-boiled tender meats and cow skin directly into the yellow soup. Let it gently warm through on low heat for 5 minutes (do not overheat or let it boil aggressively, as excessive heat can break the yellow palm oil emulsion).
