Ingredients
The Greens:

2 to 3 cups Ndolé leaves (bitter leaves) — Note: If fresh Cameroonian ndolé leaves are unavailable, frozen pre-washed ndolé or spinach can be used as a substitute, though traditional ndolé has a unique texture.

The Paste & Nuts:

3 cups raw peanuts (groundnut), shelled and blended into a smooth paste (or 2 cups of smooth peanut butter)

Proteins:

1 lb beef, smoked meat, or cow skin (kanda), pre-boiled until tender

1 cup smoked fish (cleaned and deboned)

1 cup fresh prawns or shrimp (peeled and deveined)

Aromatics & Spices:

1 large onion (finely chopped)

3 cloves garlic and a thumb-sized piece of ginger (blended)

1 cup ground crayfish

White pepper and black pepper (to taste)

Bouillon cubes (maggi/seasoning cubes) and salt to taste

Fats:

1/2 cup red palm oil (or vegetable oil, though palm oil gives it the traditional rich flavor)

Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Prepare the Ndolé Leaves
If using fresh raw bitter leaves, they must be washed thoroughly with ash or baking soda and rinsed multiple times in warm water to extract the bitterness until they are just mildly bitter to taste.

Squeeze out excess water and set aside. (If using frozen pre-washed ndolé, thaw and drain).

2. Cook the Peanut Paste
In a large pot, combine the blended raw peanut paste (or peanut butter) with about 3 cups of water or meat stock.

Bring it to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring frequently so it doesn't stick or burn at the bottom. Let it cook for about 10 to 15 minutes until the raw peanut taste cooks out and the mixture thickens.

3. Add the Greens and Meats
Add the prepared Ndolé leaves into the simmering peanut paste. Stir thoroughly.

Add your pre-boiled beef/smoked meat, smoked fish, and half of the ground crayfish.

Stir well, cover partially, and let everything simmer together for about 10 minutes on low heat.

4. Season and Fry the Aromatics
In a separate small pan, heat the red palm oil (or vegetable oil).

Add the chopped onions, blended garlic, and ginger. Sauté until fragrant and golden brown.

Pour this hot, seasoned oil directly into the simmering pot of Ndolé.

5. Add the Prawns and Final Simmer
Add the fresh prawns or shrimp and the remaining ground crayfish to the pot.

Season with white pepper, bouillon cubes, and salt to taste.

Gently stir everything together and let it simmer for another 5 minutes until the prawns are cooked through and the flavors have completely blended.
