Ingredients:
2 cans black-eyed peas (rinsed and drained) or 2 cups of dried black-eyed peas (boiled until tender)

2 to 3 ripe plantains (yellow with black spots, peeled and sliced diagonally)

1/4 cup palm oil (or vegetable oil if you prefer a milder taste)

1 large onion (finely chopped)

2 tablespoons tomato paste

2 fresh tomatoes (chopped)

Spices: 1 tsp ground ginger, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp chili powder or cayenne pepper (to taste), and 1 stock cube (Maggi or chicken)

Optional: Canned sardines or fried fish served on top for extra protein.

Instructions:
Fry the Plantains:

Heat about half an inch of vegetable oil in a skillet over medium heat.

Add the plantain slices and fry until they are golden brown and caramelized on both sides (about 2–3 minutes per side).

Remove them and let them drain on a paper towel-lined plate. Set aside.

Prepare the Stew Base:

In a separate pot, heat the palm oil over medium heat.

Add the chopped onions and sauté until they are translucent and fragrant.

Add Tomatoes & Paste:

Stir in the tomato paste and chopped fresh tomatoes. Cook this mixture for about 5 minutes, stirring frequently so it doesn't burn.

Season:

Add the garlic powder, ground ginger, chili powder, and stock cube to the pot. Stir well to combine all the flavors.

Simmer the Beans:

Pour in the black-eyed peas along with a small splash of water or bean broth.

Stir thoroughly, reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and let it simmer for about 15 minutes. This allows the beans to absorb all the rich flavors and thicken into a stew-like consistency.

Serve:

Spoon the warm, savory bean stew into a bowl and serve it alongside your sweet fried plantains.
