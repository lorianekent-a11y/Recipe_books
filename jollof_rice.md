Ingredients:
2 cups long-grain basmati or parboiled rice

4 large fresh tomatoes (or 1 can of tomato paste / blended canned tomatoes)

1 large onion (chopped)

2-3 cloves of garlic and a small piece of ginger (blended)

1-2 scotch bonnet peppers (to taste)

1/2 cup vegetable oil

Spices: 1 tsp curry powder, 1 tsp dried thyme, 2 stock cubes (Maggi/Chicken), and salt to taste

Optional: Chicken, beef, or fish (pre-seasoned and fried/baked)

Instructions:
Blend the Base: Blend the fresh tomatoes, onions, ginger, garlic, and scotch bonnet peppers together until smooth.

Fry the Stew Base: Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add a handful of chopped onions and let them fry until golden brown.

Add Tomato Paste: Pour in the tomato paste and fry for about 5 to 7 minutes, stirring constantly to remove the sour taste.

Simmer: Pour in your blended tomato-pepper mixture. Add your curry powder, thyme, stock cubes, and salt. Cover and let it simmer on medium-low heat for about 10–15 minutes until the oil starts to float to the top.

Add Rice: Wash the rice thoroughly until the water runs clear. Stir the rice into the tomato stew so every grain is coated.

Cook: Add a little water or stock (just enough to level with the top of the rice—do not over-add water). Cover tightly with foil paper and a lid to trap the steam. Cook on low heat for 25–30 minutes until the rice is tender and fully cooked.

Serve: Fluff with a fork and serve hot with your choice of fried meat, fish, or salad.

2. Red Red (Black-Eyed Pea Stew with Fried Plantains)
"Red Red" gets its name from the vibrant red color of the palm oil used in the bean stew, paired with golden-brown fried ripe plantains. It is hearty, comforting, and vegetarian-friendly.

Ingredients:
2 cans black-eyed peas (or dried black-eyed peas, boiled until tender)

2-3 ripe plantains (yellow with black spots, peeled and sliced diagonally)

1/4 cup palm oil (or vegetable oil if palm oil isn't available)

1 large onion (chopped)

2 tablespoons tomato paste

1-2 tomatoes (chopped)

Spices: 1 tsp ground ginger, 1 tsp garlic powder, cayenne pepper or chili powder (to taste), and 1 stock cube

Optional: Canned sardines or fried fish to serve on top.

Instructions:
Fry the Plantains: Heat about half an inch of vegetable oil in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the plantain slices until they are golden brown and caramelized on both sides (about 2–3 minutes per side). Remove and set aside on a paper towel-lined plate.

Make the Stew Base: In a separate pot, heat the palm oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and fry until translucent and fragrant.

Add Tomato Paste: Stir in the tomato paste and chopped fresh tomatoes. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring frequently.

Season: Add the garlic powder, ginger, chili powder, and stock cube.

Add the Beans: Pour in the cooked black-eyed peas along with a splash of water (or bean broth). Stir well, reduce the heat to low, and let the stew simmer for 15 minutes so the flavors meld together into a thick, rich consistency.

Serve: Spoon the warm bean stew into a bowl and arrange the sweet fried plantains alongside it.
