Ingredients
The Base:

2 cups Egusi (melon seeds), ground into a smooth or slightly coarse powder

Fats & Liquids:

1/2 to 1 cup red palm oil

3 to 4 cups meat stock or water

Proteins:

1 lb assorted meats (beef, tripe, or cow skin/kanda), pre-boiled until tender

1 cup smoked fish or smoked turkey, cleaned and deboned

1 cup ground crayfish

Vegetables & Aromatics:

2 cups bitterleaf (washed) or spinach / pumpkin leaves (ugwu), chopped

1 large onion (half chopped, half blended with a little water)

Scotch bonnet peppers (to taste, blended)

Salt and bouillon cubes (maggi/seasoning cubes) to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Prepare the Egusi Paste
In a mixing bowl, combine the ground Egusi with the blended onion, a pinch of salt, and a few tablespoons of water.

Mix thoroughly until it forms a thick, moldable paste. (Some cooks prefer a wet paste, while others prefer dry lumps—both work wonderfully!)

2. Form the Egusi Lumps (or Fry the Paste)
Method A (Lumps): Heat a little palm oil in a pot, then use your fingers or a spoon to drop small mounds of the Egusi paste into the pot. Let them cook undisturbed for about 5 minutes so they form firm, solid lumps. Remove them temporarily.

Method B (Direct Soup Base): Alternatively, pour the red palm oil into a large pot and heat it gently. Add the chopped onions, then stir the Egusi paste directly into the warm oil. Fry it continuously for 5–7 minutes, stirring so it doesn't burn, until it becomes fragrant and golden.

3. Add the Stock and Proteins
Pour the meat stock (or water) gradually into the pot while stirring.

Add your pre-boiled assorted meats, smoked fish, and the egusi lumps (if you used Method A).

Stir in half of the ground crayfish and the blended scotch bonnet peppers.

Cover and let the soup simmer on medium-low heat for about 15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together and the oil to float to the top.

4. Add the Greens
Add your choice of greens (spinach, pumpkin leaves, or washed bitterleaf) into the simmering pot.

Stir in the remaining ground crayfish and season with salt and bouillon cubes to taste.

Let it simmer gently for another 5 to 7 minutes until the vegetables are cooked down and tender. Do not cover the pot completely if you want to keep the vibrant green color of your vegetables.
