Ingredients
Vegetables:

2 to 3 cups of finely shredded Eru / Okok leaves (washed and drained)

4 cups of Waterleaf (finely chopped) — Note: If waterleaf is unavailable, fresh spinach makes a great substitute.

Proteins:

1 cup smoked fish (cleaned and deboned)

1 cup smoked meat or cow skin (kanda), diced and pre-boiled until tender

Fats & Liquids:

1 cup red palm oil

2 to 3 cups meat stock or water

Flavorings:

1 cup ground crayfish

Scotch bonnet peppers (to taste, blended or finely chopped)

Salt and bouillon cubes (maggi/seasoning cubes) to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Prepare the Proteins and Stock
In a medium pot, combine your pre-boiled smoked meat or cow skin with the smoked fish.

Add about 2 cups of meat stock or water, a pinch of salt, and a seasoning cube. Simmer for about 10 minutes so the proteins absorb the flavor.

2. Add the Palm Oil
Pour the red palm oil into the pot with the simmering meats and stock.

Stir well and let it cook for about 5 to 7 minutes until the oil is well integrated and bubbling. (Traditional Eru relies heavily on palm oil for its rich texture and gloss).

3. Cook the Waterleaf
Add the chopped waterleaf (or spinach) into the pot.

Stir gently and allow it to cook for about 3 to 5 minutes. The waterleaf will wilt and release its moisture into the pot. Do not cover the pot completely, as this helps keep the green color vibrant.

4. Add the Eru and Seasoning
Add the shredded Eru leaves into the pot. Because Eru is tough and chewy, mix it thoroughly with the waterleaf and meats.

Stir in the ground crayfish and scotch bonnet peppers.

Taste the soup, then add salt and bouillon cubes according to your preference.

5. Simmer and Serve
Reduce the heat to low and let the mixture simmer for another 5 to 7 minutes, stirring frequently to prevent burning at the bottom.

Once the Eru has softened to your liking (it should retain a slight, pleasant chewiness) and the liquid has reduced into a rich, oily sauce, remove it from the heat.
